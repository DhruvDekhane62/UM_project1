# Paper and executive summary rules

## Audience
Atlantic Recording Corporation A&R and marketing leadership, unless I tell you otherwise. The brief mentions "government stakeholders" for the executive summary, which looks like a copy-paste error; it is logged in `docs/open_questions.md`.

## Structure
Abstract; 1 Business context; 2 Data and method (validation steps, artist split rule, KPI definitions, units); 3 Findings, one subsection per client question; 4 Recommendations (signing, UK marketing, release format, cross-border); 5 Limitations; Appendix (KPI formulas, data quality summary).

## Language
- Describe, do not claim causes. Use "charting tracks tend to be" rather than "listeners prefer", and never "drives" or "improves" for album structure, collaboration or explicitness.
- Do not claim the UK differs from the US unless a US dataset was actually analysed.
- Every number states its denominator and unit (track-days, credits or unique songs) and cites the file in `outputs/tables/` it came from.
- If a question could not be fully answered (country data missing, no comparator), say so in the finding and again in Limitations.
- Chart titles state the takeaway with a number, for example "Top 5 artists hold 31% of chart credits".

## Executive summary
One page: three findings, three recommendations, one caveat. No jargon; define any index in one clause.
